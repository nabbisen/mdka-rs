## 1. 🛑 Required: "Parser's share" exceeds 100% on two rows

| depth | Total | `parse_document` | share |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 20,000 | 442.196 ms | **444.457 ms** | **100.5%** |
| 40,000 | 1665.205 ms | **1668.038 ms** | **100.2%** |

The parser runs inside the conversion, so its share cannot exceed 100%. `emit_depth_report` times
`total_ns` and `parse_ns` in **two independent loops**, each a median of five, so the ratio is of separate
measurements and noise inverts them on those rows.

**The measurement is sound** — ~99% of the cost is the parser, and it matches my own independent run. **The
presentation is not.** A published column labelled "share" showing 100.5% is a number that cannot be true as
labelled, and it is the first thing a sceptical reader will find.

**Your choice of fix:**
- one line saying the two columns are independent measurements, so the share is approximate (±1%); **or**
- drop the percentage column and let the two times stand.

**Do not clamp to 100%** — that invents containment the measurement does not have. If you would rather make
the containment real (time the parse inside the same run and render from the parsed tree), that is also
fine and better, but it is more than this slice needs.
