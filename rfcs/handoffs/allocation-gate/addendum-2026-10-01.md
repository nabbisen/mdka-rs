# Addendum — allocation gate, 2026-10-01

Amends `handoff.md`, which stays as issued. Review:
`.git-exclude/reviewed/allocation-gate/README.md`.

`ad4af07` is **not accepted**. The diagnosis work behind it was good — keep all of it. One property it
claims is false, and fixing it makes the gate better than the one specified.

## The defect

The bulk measurement window opens on the **first** `html_files_to_markdown` call in the process, so it
counts rayon's whole pool construction: **~7.1 KB per worker thread, ~88% of the committed
`273397 B` baseline.** The gate therefore only passes on a 32-thread machine:

| `RAYON_NUM_THREADS` | 1 | 2 | **4 (`ubuntu-latest`)** | 8 | 16 | 32 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| bulk measured | 46 318 | 53 454 | **67 726** | 96 654 | 155 695 | 273 397 |

It would go red on the first push, and on every contributor's machine.

The "bimodal 544 B step" the header treats as noise to tolerate is the same thing — per-thread
first-touch initialisation. Correctly diagnosed, then tolerated instead of excluded.

## The fix, already proven

Warm the pool before the snapshot:

```rust
let warm = mdka::html_files_to_markdown(&paths, &out_dir);
std::hint::black_box(&warm);
let before = AllocSnapshot::now();
```

I measured this: **33 925 B at 1, 2, 4, 8 and 32 threads — identical.** Do not re-derive the approach;
re-derive the numbers.

## What to do

1. **Warm the pool outside the window.**
2. **Re-derive both baselines and the `2.9.0` comparison** from the warmed measurement, in the same
   repeated-process way as before.
3. **Re-derive the bulk tolerance from the warmed spread.** Expect 0 or near it; if it is not 0, say
   what the remaining variance is and where it comes from — do not pick a round number.
4. **Paste a thread-count sweep into the file** (several `RAYON_NUM_THREADS` values, one line each).
   This is the evidence the "does not vary with machine" claim needed and did not have. **It is
   criterion 1 of this addendum**: a gate whose machine-independence is asserted rather than shown is
   the same failure in a new place.
5. **Unique temp directory per run.** The fixed `mdka_allocation_gate_in`/`_out` names collide between
   concurrent runs, and the project's convention is not the system temp dir.
6. **Correct the header**: the step was pool construction, not irreducible noise. Say so — the
   corrected reasoning is worth more than a clean-looking story.
7. **State the RFC 049 coupling in the file.** The string baseline includes `96c9a28`; the two commits
   land together, in that order.
